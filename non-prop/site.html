    The fee is your entry. You trade a simulated account with set rules. Stay in profit and inside the rules, and you can request Rewards. Break a rule and the account ends. The fee is the most you can lose.
    Where do Rewards come from?
    FundingRock pays them from its own funds, based on your simulated results. You never deposit money, and your trades never go to a real market.
    Is FundingRock legit?
    FundingRock is a trade name of Mindwave Training Limited, a company registered in Cyprus (HE 471803). You trade on TradeLocker or cTrader. Rewards are paid via Riseworks or crypto. On Trustpilot we have 3.8 out of 5 from 215 reviews.
    What if I lose?
    If you break a rule, the account ends and the fee is gone. You owe nothing more. If you haven't placed a trade, you can get a full refund within 14 days.
    I've never traded. Can I still start?
    Yes. Instant has no challenge and no profit target. Start with a $5K simulated account for $70, learn how the rules work, and the fee is the most you can lose.
    Can I use my own strategy?
    Yes. News trading and EAs (bots) are allowed. Instant has no profit target and no minimum days. Holding trades over the weekend on Instant is an add-on.
